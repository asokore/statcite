# Draft: permission request to CBB

To: statistics@centralbank.org.bb
Subject: Permission request — citing CBBWEBSTATS through an automated, attributed data service

Dear Central Bank of Barbados Statistics Team,

I maintain StatCite (https://statcite.com), a free service that gives AI assistants and researchers direct, citable access to official economic statistics. Every figure it serves carries a full citation back to the original source — publisher, dataset, series, exact table, and retrieval date — specifically so users go to the primary source rather than trusting an AI's memory of a number.

I'd like to add CBBWEBSTATS tables (Deposit Taking Financial System, Tourism, Trade in Goods, Interest Rates and Exchange Rates, GDP/Inflation/Labour, Balance of Payments) to this service, refreshed on a regular schedule, with the Central Bank of Barbados credited as the source and a direct link to the relevant CBBWEBSTATS table on every value served.

I looked for a data-reuse or terms-of-use page and didn't find one published alongside CBBWEBSTATS, so I'm asking directly rather than assuming what's permitted.

Specifically, I'm requesting permission to:
- Fetch your published CBBWEBSTATS tables on a scheduled basis (weekly or per your preferred cadence)
- Store and serve the values through StatCite's API, each one attributed to the Central Bank of Barbados with a link to the source table
- Make this available to AI agents and researchers, currently free of charge

I'm glad to follow any conditions you'd set — a specific attribution format, a request rate limit, a designated contact for questions, or anything else that works on your end. I can also send a sample of exactly what a served value and its citation look like if that's useful for your review.

Thank you for considering this, and for building CBBWEBSTATS in the first place — Barbados-specific statistics are otherwise very hard for anyone outside the country to find in a usable form.

Best regards,
