Privacy Policy

Effective: July 2026

StatCite is a read-only data service. It is designed to know as little about you as possible.

What we collect

  • No accounts. There is no signup, login, API key, or user database.
  • No request-content retention by us. Query parameters (e.g. which indicator and country you asked for) are processed to answer the request and appear in standard, short-lived aggregate infrastructure logs and metrics (status codes, latency, counts) provided by our hosting platform (Cloudflare). We do not build profiles or sell any data.
  • No cookies, no trackers, no analytics scripts on this website.

Third parties

Requests are served by Cloudflare (hosting/CDN), whose own privacy policy applies at the network level. Data queries are forwarded to official statistical APIs (World Bank, DBnomics, Frankfurter/ECB, optionally FRED) without any information about you attached — the upstream request contains only the series being requested.

Email

If you email hello@statcite.com we keep the correspondence to answer you, nothing more.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
