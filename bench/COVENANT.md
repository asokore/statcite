# Publication Covenant

StatCite self-binds, publicly, before any benchmark result exists. Violations are checkable by anyone.

1. **All outcomes are published.** Results are published with the same prominence whichever direction they point. The pre-committed interpretation grid (METHODOLOGY.md §8) governs the lead finding; if models score well, the report leads with that.
2. **The repo dataset is the canonical record.** statcite.com may summarize published waves but must link the repo. Pilot (P0) numbers never appear on statcite.com.
3. **The quotation unit is indivisible.** Within-Tolerance Rate, Confabulation Rate, and Answer Rate travel together: none may be quoted in any StatCite asset — site, social, README, talk — without the other two in the same asset.
4. **No tail anecdotes without the headline.** Marketing may never cite an individual failure ("model X was 40% off on Y") without the model's headline WTR in the same asset.
5. **`revision_affected` misses are never described as model errors**, in any asset, ever.
6. **Pilot quarantine.** P0 results are not for citation as findings about Claude or about AI, and are barred from marketing use. The first citable cross-model result requires at least two non-Anthropic vendors.
7. **Deviations are logged, never hidden.** Any post-freeze change to bank, snapshot, prompts, bands, scoring code, roster, or analysis plan goes to DEVIATIONS.md with date, cause, and effect; results tables footnote the deviation count.
8. **Neutral tone.** Per-model results are stated without dunking; failure examples always carry the model's stated confidence and error class. Covered vendors receive a 5-business-day courtesy preview (notification, not approval) from the first multi-vendor wave.
